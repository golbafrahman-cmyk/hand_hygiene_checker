# WHO reference videos / فیلم‌های مرجع WHO

## Soap-and-water handwashing / شست‌وشو با آب و صابون

- Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PmVJQUCm4E
- Identified title: "How to wash hands? With soap and water: Advice from the WHO"
- Use this as the visual reference for the soap-and-water workflow.
- WHO technique duration: 40–60 seconds.
- Core sequence: wet hands; apply enough soap; palm-to-palm; right palm over left dorsum and vice versa with fingers interlaced; palm-to-palm with fingers interlaced; backs of fingers to opposing palms with fingers interlocked; rotational rubbing of each thumb; rotational rubbing of fingertips in the opposite palm; rinse; dry thoroughly with a single-use towel; use the towel to turn off the tap.
- Do not use this video as the alcohol-based hand-rub reference.

## Implementation and validation notes

These videos are instructional references, not machine-readable ground truth. The application must not claim to verify microbiological cleanliness or complete surface coverage from an ordinary RGB video. AI outputs should be presented as candidate observations with confidence/quality indicators and require trained human review. Validate the checklist and scoring against current WHO guidance and local infection-prevention policy before operational use.
